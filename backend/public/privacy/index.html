Privacy Policy

Last updated November 28, 2026

The short version

  • When you ask for a rewrite, your message is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude AI to rewrite it. We don't save your messages.
  • The Saytwice keyboard never records your typing. It only sends text when you double-tap to rewrite.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, with RevenueCat checking who has Pro. We never see your card details.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier if you allow it.
  • We don't sell your information.

Who we are

Saytwice is a mobile app that rewrites your messages in a different tone. In this policy, "we" and "us" means the makers of Saytwice. You can reach us at apps@notacult.ca.

Messages you rewrite

When you tap rewrite, the message you typed and the tone you picked are sent over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted by Netlify), which passes them to Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, and sends the rewritten text back to you.

We do not store the text of your messages or the rewrites on our server. Anthropic processes them only to produce the rewrite. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, messages sent through its API are not used to train its AI models, and Anthropic may keep them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring as set out in Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please don't put passwords, bank details or other sensitive information into a rewrite.

The Saytwice keyboard (iPhone and iPad)

The optional Saytwice keyboard asks for "Full Access" so it can connect to the internet for rewrites. Apple shows a general warning for any keyboard that asks for this. Here is what ours actually does:

  • It does not log, store or send your keystrokes. Normal typing stays on your device.
  • It only sends text when you double-tap the mood bar to rewrite, and then it sends only the text in the box you're typing in, the same way the app does.
  • It shares a few settings with the main app through a private storage area on your device (an Apple "App Group"): your anonymous Saytwice user ID and whether you have Pro. This never leaves your device except as part of a rewrite request.
  • The keyboard does not show ads.

Your anonymous user ID and usage counts

Saytwice doesn't ask for your name, email or a login. The app creates an anonymous user ID so it can tell how many rewrites you have left. Our server stores, for each user ID, a count of rewrites used each day and any top-up credits you've bought. To stop abuse of the free version, it also counts free rewrites per IP address per day. We don't keep your IP address for anything else.

Purchases

Subscriptions and top-up packs are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and check whether you have Pro. RevenueCat receives your anonymous user ID and your purchase records from Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card details.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Pro users don't see ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, rough location, device type and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • iPhone and iPad: Apple asks whether you allow tracking. If you say no, AdMob won't use your advertising identifier and you'll see non-personalized ads.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings under Google, then Ads.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states), Google asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't read your contacts, photos or location.
  • We don't use your messages to build profiles or train AI.

Children

Saytwice is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete any related data.

Your choices and rights

You can stop using the keyboard at any time by removing it in Settings, and you can delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete the information we hold about you (your usage counts and credit balance). Email apps@notacult.ca and include your user ID if you can, and we'll help. Usage counts are only needed for the day they cover.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. For big changes, we'll let you know in the app.
