Support

Stuck on something? The answers to common questions are below. If they don't help, email us and we'll get back to you.

Contact us

Email apps@notacult.ca. Tell us what phone you have and what happened, and we usually reply within two business days.

Email support

Turn on the Saytwice keyboard (iPhone and iPad)

  1. Open Settings, then General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard and choose Saytwice.
  3. Tap Saytwice in the list and turn on Allow Full Access. The keyboard needs this to reach the internet when you ask for a rewrite. It does not record what you type (see our privacy policy).
  4. In any app, hold the globe key and pick Saytwice. Swipe the mood bar to choose a tone and double-tap to rewrite.

Restore purchases

Got a new phone or reinstalled the app? Open Saytwice, tap a locked tone to open the Pro screen, and tap Restore. Make sure your phone is signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought Pro with. If your top-up credits don't come back after restoring, email us and we'll sort it out.

Cancel your subscription

iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Saytwice and tap Cancel Subscription.

Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose Saytwice and tap Cancel.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Payments go through Apple or Google, so they handle refunds. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play refund page in your order history.

I ran out of rewrites

The free version includes 5 rewrites a day and Pro includes 50. Your count resets every day. If you need more on a busy day, top-up packs in the in-app store never expire and are used after your daily rewrites run out.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows a short ad while your rewrite is being written. Going Pro removes all ads.
